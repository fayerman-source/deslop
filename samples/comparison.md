# Plain Legal Writing: Before and After Examples

This file provides real-world examples of traditional, complex legal text ("Before") compared against plain English versions rewritten according to the directives in [SKILL.md](../SKILL.md) ("After").

---

## 1. Issue Framing (The "Deep Issue")

### Before (Traditional "Whether" Run-on)
> **Whether** or not the Appellant, having entered into a written agreement for the lease of the premises situated at 405 Elm Street, and having subsequently failed to pay rent for three consecutive months during the year 2025, can be evicted by the Landlord pursuant to Section 84-A of the State Housing Code notwithstanding the Appellant's claim of breach of the implied warranty of habitability due to a malfunctioning HVAC system.

### After (Garner's Deep Issue Format)
> Appellant Jones leased the apartment at 405 Elm Street but did not pay rent for three consecutive months in 2025. Jones claims he withheld rent because the HVAC system broke down. Section 84-A of the State Housing Code allows landlords to evict tenants for nonpayment.
>
> **Can the landlord evict Jones, or does the broken HVAC system excuse the nonpayment?**

## 2. Word Choice and Archaic Jargon

### Before (Archaic and Redundant)
> **Whereas**, the parties hereto desire to enter into a mutually agreeable arrangement, and **aforesaid** agreement shall be binding upon the heirs, successors, and assigns of **said** parties. Now, therefore, witnesseth that for and in consideration of the mutual covenants herein contained, the sufficiency of which is hereby acknowledged, the parties do hereby agree that the prior oral agreements are hereby declared **null and void and of no further force and effect**.

### After (Plain English)
> The parties agree that this contract binds their successors and overrides all prior oral agreements.

## 3. Sentence Length and S-V-O Proximity

### Before (Convoluted and Separated)
> The Tenant, within fifteen (15) days after receipt of a written invoice detailing the costs incurred by the Landlord for repairing any damage to the common areas of the building caused by the Tenant or the Tenant’s guests or invitees, shall reimburse the Landlord in full for such repairs.

*Why it fails*:
* The subject ("Tenant") is separated from the verb ("shall reimburse") by 34 words of qualifying phrases.
* The sentence is 46 words long.

### After (Direct and Close S-V-O)
> The Tenant must reimburse the Landlord for repairing any common-area damage. The Tenant must pay within 15 days of receiving a detailed invoice.

---

## 4. Nominalizations (Noun vs. Verb)

### Before (Nominalization-Heavy)
> The Committee will **conduct an evaluation of** the proposals, **make a determination as to** the winning bid, and **provide notification to** the parties **upon the completion of** the selection process.

*Why it fails*:
* "conduct an evaluation of" (evaluation is a noun instead of the verb "evaluate")
* "make a determination as to" (determination is a noun instead of "determine")
* "provide notification to" (notification is a noun instead of "notify")
* "upon the completion of" (completion is a noun instead of "complete")

### After (Active Verbs)
> The Committee will evaluate the proposals, determine the winning bid, and notify the parties when the selection process is complete.

---

## 5. Double-Entry Numbers

### Before (Double-Entry Clutter)
> The Executive shall be entitled to **thirty (30)** days of annual vacation leave and must submit a request for the same at least **fourteen (14)** business days prior to the commencement of such leave.

### After (Plain English Numbers)
> The Executive is entitled to 30 days of annual vacation leave and must request it at least 14 business days before the leave begins.
>
> *(Note: Numbers 10+ appear as digits; the doublet "(30)" is removed. Both numbers in this list are 10+, so both use digits consistently.)*

## 6. Tabulating Complex Rules (Tabulation)

### Before (Dense Condition Blocks)
> Any employee who has completed at least twelve consecutive months of employment and has worked at least 1,250 hours during the previous twelve-month period is eligible for family leave if they have a qualifying family emergency or need to care for a newborn child, or if they need to care for a spouse, child, or parent with a serious health condition, provided they submit the required medical certification within fifteen days of the request.

### After (Tabulated Format)
> An employee is eligible for family leave if the employee:
> 1. has worked for the company for at least 12 consecutive months;
> 2. has worked at least 1,250 hours during those 12 months;
> 3. submits the required medical certification within 15 days of the request; and
> 4. needs leave to:
>    a. care for a newborn child;
>    b. handle a qualifying family emergency; or
>    c. care for a spouse, child, or parent with a serious health condition.

## 7. Conversational Sentence Openers

### Before (Stuffy Transitions)
> The trial court excluded the witness's testimony. **Consequently**, the plaintiff was unable to establish the element of causation. **Moreover**, the court denied the motion for a new trial. **However**, the appellate court reversed this decision.

### After (Punchy, Flowing Conjunctions)
> The trial court excluded the witness's testimony. **So** the plaintiff could not establish causation. **And** the court denied the motion for a new trial. **But** the appellate court reversed.

## 8. Oxford (Serial) Comma

### Before (Ambiguous List)
> The package includes standard software licenses, server installation and support and maintenance.
>
> *(Note: It is ambiguous whether "support and maintenance" is a single service combined with "server installation", or if they are separate items.)*

### After (Clear List)
> The package includes standard software licenses, server installation, and support and maintenance.

## 9. Gender-Neutral Drafting

### Before (Binary Clutter)
> If a director is unable to attend the board meeting, **he or she** must notify the Chairman of **his or her** absence and designate **his or her** proxy in writing.

### After (Gender-Neutral)
> Directors who cannot attend a board meeting must notify the Chairman of their absence and designate their proxies in writing.

## 10. Omitting Needless Words (Conciseness)

### Before (Throat-Clearing and Redundancies)
> **It is important to note that** the parties must **collaborate together** in order to **completely finish** the draft agreement, and **it should be observed that** any **future plans** for modification must be in writing.

### After (Concise Plain English)
> The parties must collaborate to finish the draft agreement. Any plans for modification must be in writing.

## 11. Editorial Standards & Mechanics (ABA Publication Style)

### Before (Inconsistent Style and Punctuation)
> On **July, 2025**, the plaintiff **which** was incorporated in the **U.S.** sued **Robert Jones, III** in the **United States** District Court for recovery of **attorney's fees**. The Court stated that the contract clause was "clear and unambiguous"**;** however, it did not award the fees.

### After (ABA Style Conforming)
> In **July 2025**, the plaintiff **that** was incorporated in the **United States** sued **Robert Jones III** in the **U.S.** District Court for recovery of **attorney fees**. The Court stated that the contract clause was "clear and unambiguous"; however, it did not award the fees.

## 12. Syntactic Pitfalls & Ambiguities

### A. Eradication of "Shall"
* **Before**: The Buyer *shall* pay the purchase price upon closing. The Broker *shall* receive 3% commission, and the agreement *shall* be governed by New York law.
* **After**: The Buyer *must* pay the purchase price upon closing. The Broker *will* receive a 3% commission, and the agreement *is* governed by New York law.
* *Mechanics*: "Shall" is replaced with **must** for buyer's obligation, **will** for broker's future receipt, and **is** for the legal status of the governing law.

### B. Resolving "And/Or"
* **Before**: The Licensee is liable for any claims resulting from copyright infringement *and/or* trademark infringement.
* **After**: The Licensee is liable for any claims resulting from copyright infringement, trademark infringement, *or both*.

### C. Eradicating Provisos ("Provided That")
* **Before**: The Seller will ship the goods within ten days, *provided that* if there is a severe weather event, the shipment may be delayed.
* **After**: The Seller will ship the goods within ten days. *But if* there is a severe weather event, the shipment may be delayed.

### D. Curing Trailing Modifiers
* **Before**: The Employer will cover fees for educational seminars, certification courses, and industry conventions *held in the state of Texas*.
* **After**: The Employer will cover the following expenses *if they are held in Texas*: (1) educational seminars, (2) certification courses, and (3) industry conventions.
* *Mechanics*: Prevents ambiguity about whether "held in the state of Texas" applies to all three items or only to industry conventions.

## 13. Terms of Art Distinctions (When to Preserve)

### A. "Best Efforts" vs. "Reasonable Efforts"
* **Before**: The Developer shall use its best efforts, reasonable efforts, and good faith to complete the project on time.
* **After**: The Developer must use its **best efforts** to complete the project on time.
* *Mechanics*: "Best efforts" is the highest contractual standard (requires prioritizing this project above others). "Reasonable efforts" and "good faith" are redundant here and create uncertainty about which standard applies.

### B. "Time is of the Essence"
* **Before**: Performance by the due date is preferred but not strictly required.
* **After**: **Time is of the essence** for performance under this agreement.
* *Mechanics*: This phrase transforms time from a mere factor into a condition—failure to meet the deadline constitutes a material breach.

### C. "Act of God" in Force Majeure
* **Before**: The Contractor shall not be liable for delays caused by natural disasters, storms, floods, or acts of God.
* **After**: The Contractor shall not be liable for delays caused by acts of God, including earthquakes, floods, or hurricanes.
* *Mechanics*: "Act of God" is a recognized legal term with established meaning; expanding the list clarifies scope without diluting the term.

### D. "Representations" vs. "Warranties"
* **Before**: The Seller represents and warrants that the product conforms to specifications and will perform satisfactorily.
* **After**: The Seller **represents** that the product conforms to the specifications attached. The Seller **warrants** that the product will perform satisfactorily for 90 days.
* *Mechanics*: Representations are past or present factual statements (true when made). Warranties are promises about future performance.

## 14. Semantic Divide: Terms of Art Preservation

### A. UCC Conspicuous Disclaimer
* **Before**: The Seller makes no warranties that the products will pass without objection in the trade or are of average, acceptable quality.
* **After**: **THE SELLER DISCLAIMS THE IMPLIED WARRANTY OF MERCHANTABILITY.**
* *Mechanics*: Preserves the statutory term of art "merchantability" and uses conspicuous bold all-caps formatting, as mandated by UCC § 2-316.

### B. Burden of Proof: Lay-Facing Modernization (California CACI)
* **Before (Brief/Motion)**: The Plaintiff has the burden to establish all elements by a *preponderance of the evidence*.
* **After (Brief/Motion)**: The Plaintiff has the burden to establish all elements by a *preponderance of the evidence*. *(True term of art preserved for the court)*.
* **Before (Jury Instructions)**: To find for the plaintiff, you must find that the elements are established by a *preponderance of the evidence*.
* **After (Jury Instructions)**: To find for the plaintiff, you must find that the elements are *more likely to be true than not true*. *(Modernized for lay comprehension)*.
